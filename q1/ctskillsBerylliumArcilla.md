## Step 1: Identifying the Problem
### Main Problem
The operation in the school canteen is slow, due to most duties are manually performed.
---
## Step 2: Identify the Sub-Problems
1. The manual calculation takes too much time.
2. The availability of food items is not monitored.
3. Students have a hard time choosing what to buy.
4. Students can receive a wrong amount of change.
--- 
## Step 3: Apply Computational Thinking Skills
| Sub-Problem | CT Skill | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Manual Calculation | Algorithm Design | Create a system that automatically computes the amount. |
| Unmonitored availability of food items | Algorithm Design | Create a system that monitors and displays the items. |
| Difficulty in choosing | Abstraction | Organize the products. |
| Wrong amount of change | Algorithm Design | Create a system that automatically calculates the amount. |
